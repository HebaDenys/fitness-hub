# Project Sustainability & Funding

This document outlines the principles and potential avenues for ensuring the long-term maintenance and financial sustainability of Fitness Hub.

---

## 1. Core Commitment

The core principle of Fitness Hub is and will remain:

> **The local-first Android application is free for personal and non-commercial use.**

There are no advertisements, no tracking SDKs, and no paywalled core tracking features. The offline experience will never be crippled to force users into a paid tier or cloud service.

---

## 2. Potential Funding & Sustainability Channels

To support ongoing development, acquire testing hardware (wearables, scales, and mobile devices), and maintain dependencies, the project may explore the following avenues:

### Community Support
- **GitHub Sponsors & Individual Donations**: Voluntary recurring or one-time contributions from users who value independent, local-first health tracking.
- **Device Grants**: Hardware provided by manufacturers or community members to build and test native device connectors.

### Corporate Sponsorship & Ecosystem Support
- **Connector Sponsorship**: Organizations or device vendors funding the development and ongoing maintenance of specific open hardware connectors.
- **Privacy & Open Standards Grants**: Grants from privacy, health data, or open-source advocacy foundations.

### Commercial Licensing
- **Commercial Licenses**: Companies wishing to integrate Fitness Hub code, algorithms, or modules into proprietary commercial products under terms distinct from the personal/non-commercial source license.
- **Enterprise Support & Custom Deployments**: Professional support, custom connector integration, and consulting for research, clinical, or wellness organizations deploying local-first health stacks.

### Optional Value-Added Hosted Services (Future)
- **Hosted Sync / Encrypted Relays**: If multi-device synchronization or managed encrypted off-site backups are introduced in future phases, hosted relay infrastructure may offer an optional paid subscription. Self-hosting and local manual exports will always remain free.
